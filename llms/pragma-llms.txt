## Changelog

- [Player.gg Attribution Analytics and Player Filters](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-07-15-player-gg-attribution-and-filters/index.md): You can now see exactly which player.gg pages and traffic sources drove pageviews and signups for your game. The new Player.gg Traffic view in your Analytics dashboard breaks down your Pageview and Signup Sources across posts, lists, and other placements, so you know precisely what's sending players your way.
- [Redesigned Analytics Dashboard](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-07-13-analytics-dashboard-redesign/index.md): We've redesigned the Analytics dashboard around a new Overview that puts your most important numbers front and center. At-a-glance cards track New Players, Discord Members, Steam Playtime, Steam Wishlists, Custom Events, and Web Page Visitors, each with how they've moved versus the previous selected period, alongside cleaner charts, your top Discord channels, and a snapshot of your eight (8) latest Player and Creator signups. Same signup, Steam, and Discord data you rely on, now faster and easier to read.
- [Review Content with Submission Methods](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-07-09-campaign-submission-methods/index.md): You can now review creator content before it counts toward a campaign! We already auto-detect every matching video and stream for you, and a new Submission Method setting lets you choose what happens to that content:
- [Reposition Reward Images](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-07-01-image-positioning/index.md): You can now reposition images so they frame exactly how you want, no re-cropping or re-uploading! On a reward tier, drag to move the image and drag a corner to zoom, and set the card and the detail popup separately since they're framed differently. Image positioning also shows up in more places across the dashboard, like your creator sign-up page.
- [Pay Your Creators in Cash](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-06-29-cash-payouts/index.md): You can now pay creators in cash! Add a Cash Payout to any campaign goal and creators earn it for hitting your target, not just keys and rewards. Use it for CPM deals or any milestone you really want to drive.
- [More Languages and Localized Content](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-06-26-llm-localization-expansion/index.md): Since we launched LLM Localization, it's grown well past flows and the player dashboard. It now reaches more languages and translates almost everything you author.
- [New Creator Sign-Up and Dashboard](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-06-25-new-creator-signup-and-dashboard/index.md): We've redesigned the entire creator-facing experience, from sign-up all the way through a brand-new dashboard! Creators now get a polished home from the moment they apply to the moment they're earning. Signing up is now streamlined for creators, which means cleaner applicants for you:
- [Player Choice Keys](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-06-24-player-choice-keys/index.md): The Cross-Platform Key type has been renamed to Player Choice Keys and gained a new Custom selection type.
- [Manually Add Campaign Content](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-06-23-manually-add-content-to-campaign/index.md): You can now manually add content to a campaign by entering a URL! When ingestion misses a creator's video or stream, or the tags don't get tracked, paste the link on the campaign's Content tab and it counts toward that creator's progress like anything we detect automatically.
- [Improved Survey Response Viewing](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-06-22-survey-response-viewer/index.md): Click any row in a survey's responses table to open a drawer with that player's full submission, every question and answer in one place, including ratings, multiple-choice, and multi-select questions.
- [Twitch and YouTube Tag Tracking](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-06-19-twitch-youtube-tag-tracking/index.md): You can now specify tags, not just a game, for us to detect Twitch and YouTube content for a campaign! Set your tags in a campaign's content eligibility settings and any matching stream or video gets attributed automatically, so you catch coverage outside your game's category.
- [Redesigned Player Profile](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-06-18-new-player-profile/index.md): We've redesigned the player profile to give you a clearer, more complete view of a player in your community.
- [New Admin Navigation](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-06-17-new-admin-nav/index.md): The admin dashboard has a redesigned left-hand navigation that makes it easier to find your way around. Categories are reorganized and collapsible and sections like Players and Flows expand to reveal their sub-pages.
- [Creator Content Insights](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-06-11-creator-dashboard-insights/index.md): We've made major improvements to creator profile pages in the admin dashboard to give you a deeper understanding of your tracked creators' content.
- [Set the Default Flow](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-06-04-default-flow/index.md): You can now set any flow as the default for your game, the one players use when landing on your game's firstlook.gg URL.
- [Smart Tags](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-06-03-smart-tags/index.md): Tags can now assign themselves. Define a set of filter conditions and FirstLook will tag every player who matches, adding new players as they qualify and removing them when they don't. Make a "High Engagement" Smart Tag based on playtime and discord activity and that cohort will be kept current without anyone touching it.
- [Filter Players by Creator Campaign](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-06-02-creator-campaign-player-filters/index.md): You can now filter the Players table by which creator campaigns a player is enrolled in, and slice that further by their status in each campaign (Approved, Pending, Rejected) or by Content Amount to see how many uploads they've actually made.
- [Verify your Steam App Ownership](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-05-29-steam-app-verification/index.md): Your Steam Configuration now shows when your Steam App ownership needs verification. Verification unlocks certain Steam-related features, your listing on player.gg, and library ownership checks.
- [FirstLook Agent](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-05-20-firstlook-agent/index.md): import Video from "@components/Video.astro";
- [Improved Sentiment Filtering](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-05-18-sentiment-filters/index.md): Sentiment now has a richer filter set so you can zero in on the conversations that matter! Slice by source, language, topic, tag, and sentiment score, and narrow Discord sentiment down to a specific channel instead of the whole server.
- [Conditional Rewards](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-05-15-restrict-rewards-by-tag/index.md): You can now give some players bonus rewards inside a tier that's open to everyone! Tag a reward as restricted, and only players with the matching tags will see it; everyone else gets the rest of the tier as normal. To set it up, open any reward in your Tiers, flip on Restrict Eligibility, and add the player tags it should apply to.
- [FirstLook Command Palette](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-05-11-command-palette/index.md): You can now jump anywhere in the FirstLook Admin Dashboard with a command palette! Hit Cmd + K on Mac or Ctrl + K on Windows, type where you want to go, and the matching navigation items pop up. It also plugs into the FirstLook Agent: use /ask to talk to the agent directly, or /players to search players and run actions on them inline.
- [Player Language Selection](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-05-08-player-language-override/index.md): Players can now pick their preferred language directly from profile settings on the player dashboard! Handy for those whose system language doesn't match the one they actually want to read your game in. The choice is applied everywhere and sticks across sessions.
- [SOOP Creator Signup](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-05-06-soop-creator-logins/index.md): Creators can now link their SOOP accounts during the creator signup flow, alongside the existing Twitch, YouTube, TikTok, Kick, and Chzzk options! View creators' SOOP reach or favorite count in the creator profiles table to see which creators are driving views on the platform.
- [OR Conditions in Survey Branching](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-05-05-survey-or-conditions/index.md): Survey branching now supports OR logic! You can add multiple answers to a single branching condition and any match will trigger it; no need to set up separate branches for each one.
- [Smarter Player Queries in MCP](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-05-04-mcp-player-attributes/index.md): Our MCP integration now understands all the custom fields you've configured on your game. AI assistants like Claude, ChatGPT, and other agents that support MCP can now filter, search, and break down players by any custom question — favorite genre, playstyle preferences, age brackets, and more.
- [Auto-Enroll Creators into Campaigns](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-04-30-auto-enroll-campaigns/index.md): You can now auto-enroll creators into a campaign as soon as they're accepted into your program! In Campaign Settings, pick Auto-Enroll as the Join Method and any approved creator gets added to that campaign the moment it goes live, no opt-in click required. Best for publisher-wide campaigns where you want every creator participating by default.
- [Per-Player Key Limit](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-04-29-enable-key-limit/index.md): You can now set a per-player limit on any key type! Configure it in your Key Type settings and FirstLook will block any player from claiming more than the set number of that key, even if they unlock the reward again through a different campaign, role, or rewards tier. This is useful for studios running multiple campaigns or rewards where the same Steam, PSN, or Xbox key shouldn't go out twice to the same player.
- [Custom Agreement Names](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-04-28-rename-nda-agreement/index.md): You can now rename the NDA agreement on your flow! Studio legal teams may need their player-facing agreement to be called something more specific than "NDA", like "Beta Program Terms" or "Closed Beta Agreement". The new name shows up everywhere players see the agreement.
- [Creator Battle Pass](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-04-24-creator-battle-pass/index.md): You can now run a creator battle pass in FirstLook! We made it so campaign goals can grant Rewards points as their payout, so every milestone or performance target a creator hits moves them further along your reward tiers (Discord roles, keys, custom rewards, all of it).
- [Reach Organic Playtesters with player.gg](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-04-23-player-gg-listing/index.md): Your game can now be listed on player.gg — FirstLook's public destination where gamers discover and sign up for playtests across the network. Your game page is populated automatically from your Steam or IGDB page, so for most studios there's no extra content work: flip the toggle on and your listing is live. Players who find your game on player.gg get funnelled into your waitlist with the default flow, and show up clearly attributed in your dashboard. Confidential games are supported too — reach out to FirstLook and only your game name and self-uploaded branding will be shown.
- [Live Queue Status for Discord & Announcements](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-04-22-queue-status-notifications/index.md): You can now see live queue status for large announcement sends and Discord-throttled server adds! Studios get a Large Queue Processing banner on the admin dashboard with remaining items and ETA, and players waiting to join your Discord server see a matching notice with their estimated wait time on the dashboard and Discord tab.
- [Invite Player by Filter Conditions](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-04-21-invite-by-conditions/index.md): We reworked Invite by Role to Invite by Conditions, where instead of picking a single role, you can target invites using the same powerful filters you already use across the platform.
- [Custom Sender for Announcement Emails](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-04-20-custom-announcement-senders/index.md): For our Pro customers, you can now send announcements from a different email address than your default sender! Head to Settings, Email, and toggle on the new Announcements Sender to set its own name and address. Useful if you want your transactional emails to come from one address while announcements go out from something friendlier.
- [Playtime by Steam App](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-04-15-playtime-by-steam-app/index.md): You can now filter players by playtime for each individual Steam app being tracked in your steam settings, and see the full breakdown on every player profile.
- [AI-powered Announcements and Survey Localization](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-04-14-localized-announcements-surveys/index.md): Studios using LLM-based Localization now benefit from automatically translated announcements and surveys!
- [Capture Player Names at Sign Up](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-04-10-capture-real-name-signup/index.md): You can now ask for a player's legal name and country of residence at sign up, not just at onboarding! Toggle either option on in your Sign Up step settings.
- [Powerful Match Filter Rewards Quests](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-04-09-match-filter-quest/index.md): In Rewards, Match Filter quests just got a lot more powerful! You can now reward players based on any player table filter, not just custom onboarding question answers. Set up a quest that triggers when a player matches a specific source, has linked a certain account, hits a playtime threshold, or any combination of filters you've saved.
- [Custom Messages at Onboarding](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-04-08-custom-onboarding-messages/index.md): You can now add a custom message to your Onboarding step! Toggle on "Display a Custom Message" in your flow's Onboarding settings, then set a title and body to explain why you're asking players to link specific accounts or complete certain steps. Your custom text shows up directly in the player dashboard.
- [Cross-Platform Keys for Google Play and Apple](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-04-07-cross-platform-mobile-keys/index.md): Cross-platform keys now support Google Play and Apple TestFlight! Studios with mobile games can set up a single key distribution flow for all mobile players. Each player claims one key for their platform based on whichever account they have linked, so no keys go to waste and you don't need separate flows for each store.
- [Unity SDK](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-04-07-unity-sdk/index.md): The FirstLook SDK is now available for Unity! Drop the SDK into your Unity project to wire up runtime authentication, sessions, analytics, and in-game surveys directly from your game. Download it from the Game SDK section of your dashboard and follow the setup guide to get started.
- [Switch 2 Key Support](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-04-06-switch-2-key-support/index.md): Nintendo Switch 2 is now available as a native key type! Upload your Switch 2 keys and distribute them to players the same way you would Steam, Epic, or PlayStation keys. Switch 2 shows up as its own platform in your key management settings, complete with platform branding.
- [Publisher Audit Logs](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-04-03-publisher-audit-logs/index.md): Publisher admins can now view a full log of admin activity directly from the publisher dashboard! Every action taken by fellow admins, from privilege grants to configuration changes, is tracked and visible in one place. Built for organizations that need tighter auditing and security compliance across their organization.
- [New Hours Watched Metric for Creators](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-03-31-hours-watched-metric/index.md): Hours Watched is now available as a metric for your livestream creator campaign goals! Set a target based on total viewing time, not just viewers, to better capture the full impact of a stream. Hours Watched is calculated as average concurrent viewers multiplied by stream duration, tracked automatically across all livestreams in your campaign.
- [Custom Actions on Flows](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-04-01-custom-action/index.md): You can now add a Custom Action button to your Waitlist and Complete flow steps! Toggle it on in your flow settings and set your button text and URL. Perfect for Kickstarter campaigns, surveys, anything else you want to drive players towards.
- [Custom Announcement Email Headers](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-03-31-custom-announcement-email-headers/index.md): You can now customize the header image for individual announcement emails! By default, announcements pull your header image from Branding, but you can now upload a different image per announcement directly from the email composition page. Perfect for campaign-specific sends or events you want to stand out visually.
- [Self Player Deletion](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-03-30-self-player-deletion/index.md): You can now allow players to delete their own accounts directly from the player dashboard! Toggle this on in your game's Settings page. You can configure whether deleted players' social accounts (Discord, Steam, etc.) are unlinked immediately or put on a cooldown period to prevent misuse.
- [Masking Personally Identifiable Information (PII)](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-03-27-pii-masking/index.md): With games that have anonymization on, you can now unmask PII directly from the player dashboard! Click 'Reveal PII' on any player profile to temporarily view their information. Every reveal is tracked in player activity logs for auditing and preventing abuse.
- [Advanced Filter Operators](https://docs.firstlook.gg/changelog/2026-03-26-advanced-filter-operators/index.md): Filters on your player table now support not equals and not in operators! Click the new Advanced option in any dropdown filter (roles, state, tags, region, and more) to exclude specific values instead of only filtering for them. Need everyone except your VIP tag? Or all players not in the Waiting state? Now you can do that in one click.
